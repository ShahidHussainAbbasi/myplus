Two people on every sale

Keyed by is whoever typed it into the system (printed as “Booked By”, unchanged). Rep is who took the order from the customer — by logging in, or on paper handed to the office. Reports run on the rep. The rep is fixed on the sale when it is written; the area is the customer’s current one, so moving a customer moves their history in the report (owner’s decision).
