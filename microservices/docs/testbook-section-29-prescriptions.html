The sale was right, but the script stayed PENDING and could be dispensed again, a controlled medicine never reached the register, and the screen still said Dispense recorded. Dispenses lost in that window cannot be rebuilt from sales.

Account

owner.pharma@myplus.com. Prescriptions cannot be deleted; cancel any script you leave open.

How to dispense and complete the sale

  1. Pharmacy → Prescriptions → press Dispense on the patient's row.
  2. The till opens with a blue banner Dispensing Rx #… — patient. A Severe drug interaction box, if shown, must be answered.
  3. Item: pick the medicine. Qty: how many now. Add to Cart. Repeat per medicine.
  4. Enter Manually → the patient's name. Amount Received → what they paid.
  5. Complete Sale, then Complete Sale in the confirm box.
  6. Two green messages: Sale recorded — Invoice INV-… and Dispense recorded against Rx #….
  7. Prescriptions: the row reads PARTIALLY_DISPENSED or FULLY_DISPENSED.

What to check

  • RX-01–05 · Intake: patient and at least one line are required; quantity 0 refused; a past Valid until refused with the reason; a saved script is PENDING and moves no stock.
  • RX-06 · Cancel asks first; declining changes nothing; confirming makes it CANCELLED with no actions left.
  • RX-07 · Dispense opens the till with the banner; cancel on the banner un-links it.
  • ⚠ RX-08–10 · A full sale completes the script; a part sale leaves PARTIALLY_DISPENSED; selling more than is left records only the remainder and says so in red. This is where nothing was being recorded.
  • RX-11 · A prescription-only medicine is refused at a walk-in sale (red notice when it enters the cart) and sells through Dispense.
  • RX-12 · A SEVERE interaction must be acknowledged; Cancel backs out of the dispense.
  • RX-13 · A lapsed script reads EXPIRED, has no Dispense button, and the server refuses it.
  • ⚠ RX-14 · A controlled dispense appears on Alerts & Register with its invoice number.
