Customer Bonus Test Run

Fourteen automated cases pass — five of them added because walking this page by hand found things the other nine could not. Do the same before it goes near a real shop: P3 is the part that moves the books.

Read this before you enable it anywhere

P3 changes reported margin on every sale, not only bonus ones. Cost of goods sold moves from "the latest purchase rate" to "what the batches that actually left cost". Margin will generally fall, and that is the correction — the old figure was overstated.

So this is a per-tenant decision with finance sign-off, not a feature switch. The rollout steps are at the end of this page.

Sign in as the distribution tenant — owner.marketplace@myplus.com / Demo@2025!. Have a product with plenty of stock to hand; the steps below assume at least 60 units.

One thing that will confuse you if unwarned

Stock leaves oldest batch first (FEFO). So the cost of a sale is rarely the rate you just bought at — it is the blend of whatever older stock was actually consumed. Three of the automated cases originally failed because they expected the new rate. Do not check the cost against the rate you typed; check it against what the sale says it consumed, as the steps below do.

The defect this is why P3 exists

  1. Note the stock before

    Register → Product, and write down on-hand for your test product.

    ExampleCooking Oil 1L — on hand: 60

  2. Sell ten, give one free

    Sell → New Sale. Pick the product, quantity 10, and enter 1 in the Bonus box. Complete the sale.

    ExpectThe invoice bills TEN units. The bonus unit is shown, and is not charged.

  3. Check the shelf agrees

    Back to Register → Product.

    Expect60 → 49 (−11, not −10)

    A drop of 10 is the original defect: eleven bottles left the shop and the system recorded ten. One phantom bottle per bonus sale, for ever, never self-correcting.

The cost follows the goods this is what moves the books

  1. Look at the receipt the customer keeps

    Print or view the invoice for the sale you just made.

    ExpectThe line reads: 10 + 1 free The customer is billed for TEN.
    What you will not see, and why

    The batches a sale consumed are not shown on the Sale Detail Report or on an 80mm till slip — neither has ever had a batch column. They appear only on the A4 trade invoice, which a trade customer gets. That is unchanged by this work; the check below is the one that proves the cost is right.

  2. Check the ledger charged for eleven, not ten

    Finance → Trial Balance. Note the Cost of Goods Sold balance, make one more 10 + 1 sale, and look again.

    ExpectCOGS rises by the cost of ELEVEN units. Not ten. The free unit earns no revenue, but it still came off the shelf, so it still costs the business.

    This is the whole point of P3. If COGS rose by ten units' worth, margin is being overstated by exactly the value of what you gave away.

  3. Confirm an ordinary sale is untouched

    Sell 10 with the Bonus box empty.

    ExpectStock falls by exactly 10. The books balance. Nothing about a sale without a bonus has changed.

The counter is never blocked by a free unit

  1. Sell everything on the shelf, plus a bonus

    Find a product's exact on-hand figure — say 280 — and sell exactly that many with 1 in the Bonus box. The shop cannot cover 281.

    ExpectThe sale COMPLETES for 280 units. A message says the free goods could not be given. Stock falls by 280, not 281.
    Must NOT happen"Not enough sellable stock — 281 requested" A customer holding 280 units must never be refused because a FREE one is short.

The free unit appears everywhere it should four screens

Free goods leave the shelf and carry cost, so any screen showing a sold quantity has to say so. Four separate screens render that number, and each one was checked by hand — three of them were wrong.

  1. The till

    On Sell → New Sale, the Bonus box sits beside Quantity.

    ExpectThe box is VISIBLE and accepts a number.
    Was brokenThe box existed but was hidden by CSS. A cashier could not give free goods at all, and nine automated tests passed anyway.

  2. The sale grid

    The list under the sale form, after completing a 10 + 1 sale.

    ExpectQty reads: 10 + 1 free

  3. The Sale Detail Report

    Sell → Sale Detail Report, then View report.

    ExpectQty reads: 10 + 1 free The money columns are unchanged — a free unit is not charged.

  4. The receipt the customer keeps

    Print the invoice — on the 80mm slip and on an A4 trade invoice.

    ExpectThe line reads: 10.00 + 1.00 free Billed for TEN.

Date filters nothing to do with bonus

Found while walking this test. Three defects in how a date range was read — long-standing, and they also affected the dashboard's own numbers.

  1. A single day

    Sale Detail Report, set start and end to today, View report.

    ExpectToday's sales are listed.
    Was brokenReturned NOTHING. The picker sends a date as midnight, so "today to today" meant 00:00:00 to 00:00:00 and matched only a sale rung at exactly midnight.

  2. Current month, on the 1st

    Switch the range to Current month and View report.

    ExpectSales made EARLIER TODAY are included.
    Was brokenThe month began at the current clock time, so on the 1st of the month everything before mid-morning was excluded — and the dashboard's monthly revenue, sales count and trend chart under-reported for the same reason.

  3. The dashboard agrees

    Open the dashboard and compare the monthly figures against the report for the same period.

    ExpectThey match. Both now use the whole month.

Sign-off

  • Stock fell by 11 on a 10 + 1 sale.
  • The invoice billed 10 and showed the bonus.
  • The sale lists the batches it consumed, totalling 11 units.
  • The COGS posting matches those batches.
  • A sale with no bonus behaves exactly as it always did.
  • Selling the whole shelf with a bonus completed, without the free unit.
  • The trial balance still balances.
  • The Bonus box is visible on the till.
  • "10 + 1 free" appears on the grid, the report and the receipt.
  • A same-day date filter returns that day's sales.
  • "Current month" includes sales made earlier today.
Per-tenant rollout — required before enabling

1. Any shop that has used the Bonus column already carries a stock overstatement, because those units were never deducted. Enabling P3 will surface it, and staff will believe the new feature caused it.

2. So for each such tenant: run a physical stock count, post the difference as an adjustment reasoned "historical bonus-sale stock variance correction", record a cutover date, and only then switch the capability on.

3. A shop that has never used bonus quantities needs no correction — but it still sees the cost basis change, so finance should still sign it off.

Check the shape, do not assume

A tenant that never chose a business shape has every capability switched on by default — including bonus offers. Confirm the shape or set the capability explicitly; do not assume this only reaches distributors.
