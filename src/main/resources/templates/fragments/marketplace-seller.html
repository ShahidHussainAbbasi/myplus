Sell on the MaxTheService marketplace

List your products beside other sellers. Customers buy through MaxTheService, and MaxTheService handles their support. You keep your stock, prices and delivery area.

Seller agreement and data-sharing agreement

You sell as yourself: each sale, invoice and tax is in your books. MaxTheService earns a commission on the orders it routes to you and settles what you are owed after delivery and the return window.

Shared with MaxTheService: product identity, your marketplace price, availability, delivery area and time, warranty, return policy, and your business name.

Never asked for: what you paid suppliers, your margins, your other customers, your staff's data, or your full stock history.

Only the owner or an admin can accept these agreements for the business.
