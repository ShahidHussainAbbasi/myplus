Manual checks worth doing yourself

These are the ones a suite is structurally poor at — reachability, what a customer is handed, and whether two screens agree. Every item below is here because something in this class has already been found broken.

#21 · Returns register

  • Sidebar → Sell → Sale Returns and Purchase → Purchase Returns both open.
  • Rows show a document number (CRN-/DBN-), a party name — not an id — and a date.
  • Reprint produces a document with lines on it, not a blank page.
  • A row with no note number shows no reprint button rather than a button that fails.

#16 · Supplier filter

  • The supplier picker offers real suppliers, and only one blank option at the top.
  • Choosing one narrows the list; choosing "All" restores it.
  • Print all opens ONE print dialog, not one per note.
  • The filter is absent on the credit-note side, where it could never match.

#19 · Invoices from the report

  • Buttons appear on the Sale Detail Report toolbar.
  • They produce invoice documents — header, customer, lines, totals — not a dump of the grid.
  • A three-line sale yields ONE invoice, not three copies of it.
  • Download gives one PDF per invoice, and asks first past ten files.

#20 · Stock value tile

  • The tile sits beside the product count and shows a number, not a dash.
  • Its label says "at last purchase rate". An unqualified "stock value" would be read as a books figure and will not match the ledger.
  • It changes after a purchase.

#18 · Report by company

  • A Company filter appears on the report, offering only companies the data contains.
  • Choosing one narrows the rows; an impossible one returns nothing rather than everything.
  • Group by Company produces subtotals.
  • The CSV export carries the filter — otherwise it silently exports the whole month.

tier 1c · Sale screen speed

  • Opening New Sale lets you type into the item box before the sales list has finished loading.
  • The customer picker fills in while the screen is already usable.
  • Saving a purchase line does not rebuild the pickers under you mid-entry.

Before any tenant gets P3

Per-tenant, with finance sign-off

P3 changes reported margin on every sale, not only bonus ones. And any shop that used the old Bonus column already carries a stock overstatement, because those units were never deducted — enabling P3 surfaces it, and staff will blame the feature.

Run a physical stock count, post the difference as an adjustment reasoned "historical bonus-sale stock variance correction", record a cutover date, then enable.

Check the shape, do not assume

A tenant that never chose a business shape has every capability on by default, bonus offers included. This does not only reach distributors.
