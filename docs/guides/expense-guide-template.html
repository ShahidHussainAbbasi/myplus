FP-6a · The automatic daily check

Every business is checked by itself, every night at 03:30 (Karachi) and 10 minutes after every start: business’s supplier figures against finance’s supplier documents, and those against the books’ 2000 Accounts Payable. A difference is repaired automatically — documents re-sent, then the books aligned against 2990 Payables Reconciliation Difference — and the day is recorded. After 28 clean days in a row a business can stop keeping its own payables (FP-6b). The operator sees the history; nobody has to press anything.

TZ-2 · “Today” is your day, at any hour

The servers keep time in UTC, five hours behind Pakistan. Until this fix, between midnight and 05:00 the server still thought it was yesterday. It refused an expense dated today as “in the future”, defaulted payments to yesterday, and finance reports opened on the last day of the previous month. Now your browser tells the server which time zone you are in. The server works out “today” in that zone, and the screens build the date from your own calendar. You do not need to wait for midnight to test this: changing your computer’s time zone moves “today” in exactly the same way.
